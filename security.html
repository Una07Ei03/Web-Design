Security & Data Privacy

At ClinkNote, we prioritize the integrity and confidentiality of your medical data. Our architecture is engineered to meet the highest international standards for clinical research and digital health data security.

1. Patient-Centric Data Sovereignty

Unlike centralized Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, ClinkNote utilizes a decentralized data logic. Medical reports are encrypted on the client side, ensuring that the legal ownership of data remains exclusively with the patient, enabling cross-border and cross-institutional portability.

2. Advanced Encryption Standards

All data in transit is protected via SSL/TLS encryption. Data at rest is secured using AES-256 encryption, adhering to HIPAA requirements for Protected Health Information (PHI) storage.

3. Regulatory Compliance & Audit Trails

In alignment with 21 CFR Part 11, ClinkNote maintains comprehensive audit trails. Every interaction—from AI-driven OCR extraction to data modification—is timestamped and logged, ensuring data traceability and ALCOA+ (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate) principles.

4. Closed AI Ecosystem (RAG Architecture)

Our proprietary AI analysis operates within a closed-loop system. We utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ensure that user data is never used for general model training, maintaining absolute privacy while providing precise, evidence-based insights.

5. Intellectual Property Notice

The methodologies for cross-institutional data normalization and decentralized health data management within ClinkNote are currently under Patent Pending status and protected as trade secrets.
